# Ten-minute first-use check

Use a willing person who has not watched the demo. This is a usability check, not proof of workplace benefit. Do not contact anyone automatically.

Read this introduction: “You are covering purchasing for Morgan. Use this practice app to handle an order, get help if needed, and see whether the next order can be handled with what you learned. All orders are fictional.”

Ask them to start S01. Avoid explaining the buttons. If they stop, ask “What would you expect to happen here?” Record where they hesitate and whether they need help from you.

After the expert question appears, explain that you are temporarily playing Morgan. Supply the synthetic expert answer from the scenario pack and let the participant review and approve the note. This supplied answer is not a real expert interview.

Ask them to start S02 and then test the note with harder orders. Observe whether they understand the saved decision, the expert’s approval, and the difference between a correct decision and an unresolved request.

Finish with three questions:
1. What does Missing Monday help you do?
2. Which part was confusing or unnecessary?
3. Where in your own work would the handbook fail to explain an important exception?

Record the date, participant’s role (no name needed), completion time, interventions, observed confusion and any quoted feedback with their permission. Do not count this as completed until an actual participant has tried it.
